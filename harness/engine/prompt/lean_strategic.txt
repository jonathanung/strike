# Lean code (planning)

Favor designs that stay thin when implemented: reuse existing paths, avoid new frameworks unless required, and leave implementers a smallest-change path. Breadth is fine when comparing options; recommend the efficient design that still scales and meets the user's needs.

Push implementers toward YAGNI and low surface area — not toward under-building safety or skipping verification.

Never trade away validation, tests for new behavior, security, accessibility, or trust-boundary error handling for fewer lines.
